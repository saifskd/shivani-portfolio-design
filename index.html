About Me

In which our visual creator is introduced.

Panel 1 — The Creative Eye

I’m Shivani Jain, a graphic designer based in Delhi with over a year of experience creating social media creatives, marketing materials, and brand identity assets. I enjoy combining typography, color, and layout to turn ideas into clear, engaging designs. My work spans digital and print, from promotional posts and product visuals to editorial layouts and short-form videos.

“Good design starts with a clear idea.”

My Creative Approach

  • 01Understand the message — Identify the core communication goal, audience, and brand personality.
  • 02Explore visual directions — Experiment with dynamic layout, color palette harmony, and expressive typography.
  • 03Refine the details — Polish alignment, contrast, visual hierarchy, and precise spacing.
  • 04Deliver consistent designs — Ensure unified brand aesthetics across digital feeds, print collateral, and video.
